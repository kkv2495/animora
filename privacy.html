Privacy policy

Last updated: 02 November 2026

Who we are

This website is operated by Leo Future Software Solutions ("we", "us"), which runs the brand Leo Creative Studios. We are based in Telangana, India. If you have any question about this policy, write to info@leocreativestudios.com.

What we collect

We only collect the information you choose to give us, mainly through the enquiry form or when you email or message us:

  • Your name, email address and contact number
  • Your company or organisation
  • Details about your project, budget and timeline
  • Any files or references you send us

How we use it

We use this information to reply to your enquiry, prepare proposals and quotes, deliver projects you engage us for, and keep records required for invoicing and tax. We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Your browser may store small technical settings (for example, whether the intro animation has already played) that never leave your device.

Sharing

We share your information only with service providers we use to run our business (such as email and file-sharing services), with team members working on your project, or when the law requires it.

Retention and security

We keep enquiry information for as long as needed to respond and for any project that follows, and project records for as long as the law requires. We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by writing to info@leocreativestudios.com. We will respond within a reasonable time, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable laws.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
